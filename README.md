# Handcrafted Skincare & Body Care Brand Store

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FOlumidenana%2Fskincare-brand-store)

A luxury botanical storefront and dispatch manager designed for **Handmade Skincare, Whipped Shea Butter, Organic Soaps, and Glow Oil Creators**.

### Features:
- 🧴 **Artisanal Product Catalog**: Showcase butters, body polishes, hair drops, and glow elixirs with high-res photos.
- 📦 **Smart Zone Shipping Calculator**: Automatic delivery fees for local island/mainland, regional cities, and nationwide delivery.
- 💳 **Paystack Payment**: Full card and bank transfer checkout before batch dispatch.
- 📊 **Google Sheets Dispatch Sheet**: Automatically organizes deliveries by address, landmark, and customer phone for riders.
- 💬 **Instant Telegram & WhatsApp Dispatch Alerts**: Direct ping when an order is queued.
- 🛠️ **Owner Admin Portal**: Live order management at `/admin`.
